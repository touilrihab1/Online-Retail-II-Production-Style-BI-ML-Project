## Phase 2 — Feature Engineering & Churn Model

- [ ] Build a feature table from RFM + cohort + product-diversity features (e.g., number of distinct categories bought, avg order value, days since first purchase)
- [ ] **Time-aware split, not random split**: train on behavior up to month T, predict churn in T+1 (prevents label leakage from future purchases)
- [ ] Baseline model: Logistic Regression
- [ ] Main model: XGBoost
- [ ] **Use Bayesian Optimization (not grid/random search) to tune XGBoost hyperparameters** — direct, honest tie-in to your thesis work; write a paragraph explaining why BO is more sample-efficient here
- [ ] Evaluate with precision/recall/F1/PR-AUC — explain why raw accuracy is misleading given class imbalance
- [ ] Add a short calibration check (reliability curve or Brier score) on the churn probabilities — ties back to your Bayesian background
- [ ] Feature importance / SHAP for interpretability

**Acceptance criteria:** A documented, time-aware churn model with a justified tuning method and honest evaluation (not just accuracy), plus an interpretability step.
